Privacy Policy for Udhaya Netram (ఉదయ నేత్రం)

Last Updated: September 2026

Udhaya Netram (ఉదయ నేత్రం) is a daily Telugu digital newspaper and news portal operating from Amalapuram, Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Konaseema District, Andhra Pradesh. We are committed to protecting your personal privacy.

1. Information We Collect

The Udhaya Netram application is designed as a digital newspaper reader. We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information to read news or download daily e-papers.

  • Device Information: General device details (OS version, screen resolution) for layout optimization.
  • Storage Access: When you download daily PDF e-paper editions, the app utilizes Android's DownloadManager to store the PDF in your device's Downloads directory.

2. App Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to fetch the latest Telugu breaking news and digital editions.
  • DOWNLOAD_WITHOUT_NOTIFICATION / STORAGE: Required to download PDF e-papers to your phone.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use standard analytics and hosting infrastructure (such as Google Firebase or GitHub Pages) to deliver content efficiently. These third-party services may collect standard anonymous web traffic data.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our app does not collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The news content provided is general news, community updates, and digital e-paper editions.
